I’m Mingchao Wang.

I work between design research, cultural knowledge and image-making. My practice brings together creative systems, fashion imagery and moving image—turning complex ideas into forms people can explore and experience.

Through contextual inquiry, knowledge organisation, prototyping and evaluation, I examine how creative decisions are made, and what we can learn by making. This archive brings the research and the work into the same conversation.

APPROACH · 001—005

METHOD
AS PRACTICE.

Not a catalogue of tools. Five ways of turning complex material into researchable questions, testable forms and considered decisions.

SELECTED PUBLIC EVIDENCE · NO UNPUBLISHED RESULTS
RESEARCH / CONNECTING QUESTIONS

KNOWLEDGE, INTERACTION
AND JUDGMENT.

How does knowledge shape creative decisions? My research develops design-support systems and examines how people interpret, modify and evaluate the possibilities they encounter. The five capabilities below are complementary practices, not a single universal workflow.

01 / SITUATED KNOWLEDGE

What needs to remain meaningful?

Describe forms in their cultural and material contexts; organise evidence so that reference is more than a recognizable motif.

02 / CREATIVE SUPPORT

What changes in the act of making?

Translate research needs into accessible information and testable interactions while keeping creative control with the user.

03 / DESIGN JUDGMENT

How should alternatives be assessed?

Examine decisions, experiences and resulting artefacts as related but distinct evidence of a design process.
